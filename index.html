01 / About

Finance meets purpose

I'm a penultimate master's student at Northumbria University London studying Business with Financial Management, built upon a first master's in Applied Economics & Sustainability from the Norwegian University of Life Sciences.

My edge sits at the intersection of rigorous financial modelling and ESG analytics — a combination increasingly critical as capital markets price climate risk. I've applied STATA panel regression to 355 UK firms to map ESG-to-performance linkages, built multi-scenario DCF models for renewable energy projects, and translated sustainability data into measurable P&L impact.

Currently developing a dissertation on the Carbon Risk Premium and UK stock returns. Fluent in English and Vietnamese, working proficiency in Norwegian.
