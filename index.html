Background

Where finance meets purpose

I'm a penultimate master's student at Northumbria University London studying Business with Financial Management, building on a first master's in Applied Economics & Sustainability from the Norwegian University of Life Sciences.

My edge lies at the intersection of rigorous financial analysis and sustainability strategy — an increasingly valuable combination as markets price in climate and ESG risk. I've applied DCF modelling to renewable energy projects, conducted panel regression on 355 UK firms to explore the ESG–financial performance link, and built commercial finance dashboards in fast-paced business environments.

Fluent in English and Vietnamese, with working knowledge of Norwegian. Analytical tools include Excel, Python, R, STATA and Power BI. TCFD-certified. Currently working on a dissertation exploring the Carbon Risk Premium and UK stock returns.
